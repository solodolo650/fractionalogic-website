# Inner pages: About, Services (FCMO, Twinning), Partners (Strategic, Twin Network), Resources, Talk to Us

Scope: the seven inner routes, inheriting the established homepage world (Executive Logic palette, Inter / Inter Display, teal tritone photography, 8px radius, 1060px column). Extension, not a new world.

Visitor modes: About = Read. FCMO, Twinning, Strategic Partners = Persuade. Twin Partner Network = Persuade (secondary audience: operators). Resources = Read/Operate (find and download). Talk to Us = Operate (complete the form).

## Direction contract

OWN-WORLD: Warm Porcelain ground, Logic Teal 950 headings in Inter Display 600, Teal 650 actions, Logic Mist panels, Silver hairline rules, teal-tritone dithered photography in closing bands and feature tiles (never a full-width mid-page band), Brass reserved for recognition.

STORY: The visitor learns what happens (sequence), who does it (founder, partners), what it is worth (sourced numbers), then acts through one consistent "Book an FCMO Conversation" route to the Talk to Us form.

FIRST VIEWPORT: Left-aligned breadcrumb, Inter Display page title at 3.5–4rem, a one-paragraph lede at 60ch, primary action within the first viewport on Persuade pages; the page's signature structure starts above the fold on desktop.

FORM: Structure delegated by the user in their own words: "take the best possible UI and UX decisions to build out these pages… review, look at it, take decisions, and build it out." A written per-page structure plan was shown before their answers and not objected to; no concept-seed roll was run on that basis.
