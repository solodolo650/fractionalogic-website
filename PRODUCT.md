## Platform

web

## Users

Primary buyer: the founder, CEO, or managing director of a growth-stage B2B SaaS, AI, enterprise technology, or tech-enabled business, especially one building from India or scaling across markets. They own the growth number and have outgrown founder-led marketing: traction exists, but positioning is soft, marketing activity is scattered, the pipeline is unpredictable, or the team needs senior direction. Board members are a plausible secondary reader.

A VP of Marketing is explicitly not a target buyer.

Secondary audience: senior operators (CROs, CFOs, CTOs, COOs, specialist marketers) building a fractional practice who may join the Twin Partner Network.

## Product Purpose

Fractionalogic is a Fractional CMO practice founded by Muralidharan Erumbala. It embeds revenue-accountable, evidence-led senior marketing leadership into a client's leadership team, part-time in hours and full-time in ownership, then extends into a broader fractional leadership system through Fractional Twinning as the client's needs cross functions.

Success on the site: a founder books an FCMO conversation through the Talk to Us form, or downloads a report; a senior operator applies to the roster.

## Positioning

"Senior leadership logic, applied fractionally." One practice, one path: the FCMO is where every relationship starts, and Twinning pairs a complementary fractional executive around one shared outcome, with shared KPIs, unified reporting, and joint accountability rather than parallel advisers. Not arm's-length consulting, not an agency running disconnected campaigns, not a part-time executive filling a calendar.

## Operating Context

- Engagements are retained or project-based, typically two to four days a month at leadership level, and sequence Diagnose, Decide, Build, Sustain.
- Strategic partner Magic Pencils (Chennai) supplies readiness, collective-intelligence, and capability instruments: Momentum Zone, Huddle, Skill Forge. C3 (Consult, Coach, Conquer) and R4 (Repair, Replenish, Recreate, Relaunch) are internal delivery frameworks.
- Home base India; served regions APAC, Middle East, UK, US via the vetted Twin Partner Network.

## Product Principles

1. Evidence before assumption: every claim is sourced or removed.
2. Diagnosis before delivery: the first conversation is an honest read, not a pitch.
3. Extension, not dilution: grow by adding complementary leadership, never by stretching one leader thinner.
4. Capability that outlives the engagement: the client keeps what is built.
5. The FCMO is always the entry point; Twinning is the extension path.
